## Overview

Analysed consumer preferences, brand awareness and competitor positioning for CodeX, a German beverage company entering the Indian energy drink market.

## My contribution

- Analysed 10,000 consumer responses across 10 Indian cities.
- Examined consumer demographics, purchasing behaviour and brand perception.
- Conducted competitor benchmarking and evaluated product preferences, including flavours, packaging and pricing.
- Identified opportunities for regional marketing and influencer-led campaigns.
- Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard and presented strategic recommendations.
