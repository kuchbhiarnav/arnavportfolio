## Overview

Developed a tourism analytics solution using publicly available Telangana government datasets, with a focus on domestic and foreign tourist activity and regional performance.

## My contribution

- Analysed domestic and foreign tourist trends across Telangana's districts.
- Compared district-level performance using Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR).
- Identified peak and low-performing tourism months.
- Examined Hyderabad's tourism trends and developed visitor and revenue projections.
- Translated analytical findings into recommendations for tourism planning and administration.

## Deliverables

- Interactive Power BI Dashboard
- Insight CSV Files
- Research Presentation

**Data source:** [Open Data Telangana](https://data.telangana.gov.in/)
